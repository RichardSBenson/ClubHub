# Classes and attendance

**Classes** are the times a club trains — its timetable, edited on the club's page
(*Club page → training times*). Nothing is duplicated: the roll reads that list.

**Attendance** is who came. Instructors, registrars and administrators of a club
open **Attendance**, choose the day (today by default), and **Take the roll** for
each class that runs that day: tick who came, and add visitors from other clubs
by member number. Saving sets the roll to exactly who is ticked, so changing your
mind or taking it twice is safe. Nobody is marked *absent* — the record is the
people who turned up.

Rules:

- Only the club's own instructors and above, only that club's classes.
- Not for a class that has not happened yet, not for a day the class does not run,
  and not more than 60 days back — a roll made up months later is not a roll.
- A **visitor** must be an active member of another club in the same federation.
- Every roll taken is in the history (who, which class, which day, how many).

What it is for:

- **Grading eligibility.** "Sessions since the last grading" counts these rows, so
  keeping the roll is what makes a grade's attendance requirement work.
- **A person's record** shows classes in the last 30 and 90 days and when they were
  last seen, to the people who look after them.
- **Not seen in 30 days** lists members worth a kind word. It is not a penalty, and
  a brand-new member is not listed until they have had time to be missed.

Not built yet: classes that are not on the weekly timetable (a one-off seminar
session), a member checking themselves in, and attendance for children being
visible to their parents.
