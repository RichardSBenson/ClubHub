# Qualifications and compliance

A grade is permanent; first aid, police vetting and child protection training are not. This answers "who may not teach right now?" from dates.

- **What is tracked** (Compliance → What is tracked): the federation (owner/administrator) defines qualifications — name, kind, how many months valid, and what each is **required for**: teaching a class, judging, or sitting on a grading panel. One click adds starters (first aid, police vetting, child protection, instructor certificate, referee) — their validity periods are suggestions to confirm. Clubs inherit the federation's list.
- **Recording**: a registrar opens a person (Qualifications and checks) and records issue date, optional issuer and reference. The expiry fills from the usual period unless set by hand. Only the *fact and dates* are held — never what a check found. A renewal supersedes the older record; the latest expiry counts.
- **Who is cleared**: an instructor or assistant is cleared when every qualification required for teaching is current or never expires. Expiring (within 60 days) is a warning; expired or not recorded is a bar. The compliance screen lists those not cleared and everything running out. A federation sees every club beneath it.
- **Enforced, not just reported**: a qualification required for *panel* is checked when a grading is finalised — an examiner without it is refused by name and reason, and nothing is awarded. (Teaching is reported, not blocked: classes are not refused.)
- **Reminders**: opt-in per club (administrator). Daily, each award is reminded about once when it enters its 60-day window and once when it lapses (not for ones lapsed over 90 days). The person is written to (guardian for a minor) and the club's owners, administrators and registrars get a list. Sent as the club, as service messages.
- **Seeing your own**: members and parents see their current qualifications on their page; only registrars and above can record or delete.
- **Report**: "Qualifications and compliance" in Reports downloads it.
- **Not built**: uploading the certificate document itself.
